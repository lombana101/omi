# First use checklist

Do this only while you are alone.

1. Install from `INSTALL.md` and open the privacy app from the Home Screen. Recording must say off.
2. Test the connection. It must not start capture.
3. Choose retention and enter a purpose. Assert self-only.
4. Connect the pendant. Button notifications should be on and audio unsubscribed before you start.
5. Start, say one short non-sensitive sentence, and stop. Confirm authorization happened before audio.
6. Reopen the app. The transcript should remain and recording should be off.
7. Save a correction in the correction field and a separate owner note.
8. Create a task from that chunk, edit the title before saving, then edit the task, assign a project you select, set a due date and time, complete it, and reopen it.
9. Accept or edit-and-accept a suggestion. Confirm it is labeled as a suggestion until you accept.
10. Delete the session. Choose whether to delete linked tasks or keep the commitment. Confirm the receipt says deleted from this phone and remote deletion is unavailable.
11. Background or force-quit during a second self-only capture. Recording must be off and must not resume.

Do not record another person. Do not enter a firmware update flow.
